How Meridian works

Meridian fuses two views of a company:

  1. Base Fair Value (BFV) — a transparent estimate from sector-median multiples (P/E, EV/EBITDA) and a conservative DCF-lite.
  2. Sentiment Index (0–100) — a blend of news tone (GDELT + AI) and a macro / geo-political overlay.

They combine into Sentiment-Adjusted Fair Value:

adjustment = (SentimentIndex − 50) / 50
SAFV = BFV × (1 + k × adjustment)

k is capped so sentiment only tilts a fundamentally-grounded value. On the Sector Signals tab, the AI decides direction and writes the narrative, but the forecast magnitude is a historical volatility band — how the sector has actually behaved in past events of that type — so figures are anchored, and shown as a range rather than a false-precision point estimate.

⚠ Educational / research instrument. Data refreshes daily, not in real time. Forecast ranges are historical-analogue estimates, not predictions. Not financial advice.
